About us

We're a small team of designers and builders who believe a good garden should feel effortless: somewhere to eat, play and unwind, that still looks good in February.

  • i.

    One team, start to finish

    The people who design your garden are the people who build it. No hand-offs, no subcontractor surprises.

  • ii.

    Fixed, written quotes

    One clear price covering materials, labour and waste removal, agreed before work begins.

  • iii.

    Built for British weather

Services
